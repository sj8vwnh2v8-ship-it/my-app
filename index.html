          Add Ember to your Home Screen

          Ember is a to-do list that builds a daily streak. It works like a normal app once it's on your Home Screen, and it's free with no account.

          1. 1Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari
          2. 2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
          3. 3Tap Add, then open Ember from your Home Screen

          Please open it from the Home Screen icon. A list started here in Safari won't carry over to the app.
